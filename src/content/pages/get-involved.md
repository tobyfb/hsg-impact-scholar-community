## Become a steward

We founded the community during our own PhDs, which concluded a while ago. We are now in the
process of handing over stewardship to a new group of PhD students who want to co-shape the
future of the community. If you would like to join them, please reach out.

What this looks like:

- Co-organise at least one main community event per year.
- Keep the community alive with regular updates on relevant developments.
- Everything else is up to you, based on your capacity and drive. Bring your own ideas!

Being active in the community is a great way to shape your own PhD journey. You build your
network across disciplines, get in touch with experts at HSG and beyond, gain visibility for
your ideas, and develop new skills along the way.

You can also take on one part instead of the whole:

- **Events:** develop and co-create events and formats with the community, and look after
  their logistics.
- **Communication:** send the newsletter, keep this website up to date, manage the LinkedIn
  group.
- **Community development:** position and develop the community, find partners and
  collaborators.
- **Co-creation:** support or run a single activity, or bring in ideas for events.

Interested or just curious? [Get in touch](#contact) without any obligation, or talk to us at
the [Community Get-Together on 30 November 2026](/events/2026-11-30-mini-methods-jam/).

## Host the community

In a hosted-by event, your chair, institute or team invites the community for an afternoon.
Your researchers pitch their projects, and the group discusses them in a world café, usually
followed by an apéro. If you would like to host, write to us and we plan it together.

## Present your work

The Methods Jam, the Academic Poster Session and the Conference Practice Space are open
stages. If you work on an important topic with an interesting method, or want to rehearse a
talk in front of an engaged audience, let us know.

## Share news

We are happy to share news related to impact-oriented research at HSG in the newsletter:
events, calls, publications, new courses. Send us a few lines and a link.
