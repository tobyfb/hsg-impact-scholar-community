## What we do

We want to create connections and synergies across institutes, breaking the silos and
strengthening a collaborative research culture at HSG. The community is a space for dialogue
on how research can create impact on society and the natural environment. We connect people
in the HSG ecosystem and share knowledge and experiences.

Over the years a few formats have become regular:

- **Hosted-by events:** a chair or institute hosts the community, its researchers pitch their
  projects, and a world café follows.
- **Methods sessions:** workshops on quantitative and qualitative sustainability research, and
  since 2026 the Methods Jam with short method spotlights.
- **Academic Poster Session:** researchers present SDG-related work on posters, together with
  partners at HSG.
- **Conference Practice Space:** members rehearse their conference presentations and get
  feedback.
- **Semester kick-offs and holiday events:** time to reconnect and meet new people.

All of them are listed under [events](/events/).

## Who it is for

Early career researchers and everyone else at HSG working on social or environmental
innovation, grand challenges, sustainability, responsibility, ethics, systems thinking or
related topics. We understand "member" very broadly: there is no formal membership. If you
read the [newsletter](/newsletter/) or come to an event, you are part of it.

## Part of a global community

Both the concept and the name of our local community were inspired by the global
[Impact Scholar Community](https://www.impactscholarcommunity.com), which was established in
2020, is supported by the Academy of Management (AOM) and hosted by its Organizations and the
Natural Environment division. Members of our community have taken part in its mentoring
programme and co-organise joint virtual sessions.

## How it started

The community was initiated in May 2021 by early career researchers at the Competence Center
for Social Innovation (CSI-HSG), the Institute of Management and Strategy (IfB-HSG) and the
Institute for Economy and the Environment (IWÖ-HSG): Felicitas Pietrulla, Miron Avidan,
Stephanie Frick, Tabea Bereuther and Tobias Fehr-Bosshard. Their invitation read:

> We want to create connections and synergies across institutes (breaking the silos),
> strengthening a collaborative HSG research culture. Let's exchange, collaborate, develop
> cool things, learn from and support each other!

About twenty researchers followed it to the
[kick-off on 6 July 2021](/events/2021-07-06-community-kick-off/), where a World Café collected
what impact means to them and what they expected from a community. In 2026 the community
turned five.
