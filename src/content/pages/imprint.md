## Your data on this website

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. Fonts and images are served
from the website itself; nothing is loaded from other providers.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. As with any website, the hosting provider processes
technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages.

Some links lead to services outside this website, and their own privacy terms apply there:

- the newsletter sign-up form (Brevo),
- sign-up forms for events (Microsoft Forms),
- LinkedIn posts and the LinkedIn group.

For the University of St.Gallen in general, see its
[data protection declaration](https://www.unisg.ch/en/data-protection-declaration/).

## People and photos on this website

Facilitators, collaborators and supporters are listed on the people page with their
agreement. Photos show community events.

Would you like your name, your profile or a photo of you changed or removed? Write to us and
we will do it promptly. You do not need to give a reason.
