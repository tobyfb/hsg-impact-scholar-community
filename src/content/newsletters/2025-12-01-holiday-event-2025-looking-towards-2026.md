Dear Impact Scholar

The end of the year is quickly approaching (even though it seemingly only just started)!

With this email we want to invite you to our traditional Holiday Event as a last opportunity
to connect and enjoy each other's company in the old year – and to envision an exciting 2026
together.

On short notice, please also see the opportunity to create visibility for an exciting project
you are working on at tomorrow's Sustainable University Day.

Hope to see you soon and warm regards,
Eugenia, Tabea, and Tobias for the HSG Impact Scholar Community

## Community events

### Impact Scholar Community Holiday Event

10 December 2025, 16:30–18:00 · Room 61-052 at [Rosenbergstrasse 30](https://maps.app.goo.gl/K9EBbooirThgSoFo9) (close to the train station)

As every year, we want to create a space to **share gratitude** and things we are currently
excited by (in work, research, life). We also want to experiment with **collaboratively
planning the year 2026** for the HSG Impact Scholar Community. By now, we have a few
established formats (semester openings, Academic Poster Session, Conference Practice Space,
Hosted-by Events) but also always hope to co-create new events. What would you be particularly
interested in? And is there something you might even commit to
co-organizing/contributing to/shaping in 2026? Finally, we'll say goodbye to the amazing
**Eugenia Bajet Mestre** 🙌 who has been such a key collaborator in developing our community
over the last few years.

There will be **Glühwein** and snacks! (as well as non-alcoholic Punsch) ☕️🤗

👉 Please **sign up** so we have an appropriate amount of drinks and snacks

Last but not least: This is a nice opportunity to get a sense of the community for anyone who
is interested in impact scholarship and community but might not have participated in the past.
We warmly invite you to **invite colleagues and friends**! 💜

We look forward to connecting once more and toasting to 2025 together!

## From the HSG ecosystem

### Short notice: Make your work and projects visible at the Sustainable University Day

2 December 2025 · Rapperswil

Tomorrow, on December 2nd, the
[Sustainable University Day 2025](https://www.sustainable-university-day.ch/) takes place in
Rapperswil. If you want to **make your own work more visible** and showcase the exciting
things related to sustainability and impact at HSG, the
[St.Gallen Transformation Lab](https://transformation-lab.co/) has a poster and stand, where
you can do so.

Feel free to send an A4 or A3 flyer 🚨 **by the end of the day** (this can be the abstract of
an article, a slide from a course or just a printout of a LinkedIn post) to Tobias.
