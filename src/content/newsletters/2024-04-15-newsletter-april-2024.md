## Community events

### Mock AOM 2024 session

13 June 2024, 14:00–17:00

Quite a few of our community members got accepted for presentations at
[AOM 2024 in Chicago in August](https://aom.org/events/annual-meeting/future-annual-meetings/2024-innovating-for-the-future-policy-purpose-and-organizations).
For a few of us it's the first time being and presenting at AOM. Yet, HSG has a large number
of experienced AOM attendees and presenters. Our idea is to have a session mid-June that
helps us in preparing and exchanging about AOM. We envision two parts: **1. What I wish I had
known during my first AOM** where experienced members share a few of their key learnings and
best practices for attending conferences in general and AOM in particular. **2. "Mock"
presentations** by attendees to test their presentations and get feedback from peers and
faculty. The idea is that this is not only a way to prepare for AOM individually but also an
opportunity to get to know other HSG people and their research.

Who: first time AOM (or other conferences) attendees, experienced presenters and everything
in between

How: We'll define the exact format once we know who and how many people will attend

Please sign up and indicate if you could contribute a short input (3-10 minutes) on something
first-time attendees should know (best practices, how to prepare, who to talk to, …) and/or
if you would like to participate with a mock/training presentation and/or would be willing to
provide feedback on presentations.

### New Certificate Programme in Sustainability for Bachelor Students

The Institute for Economy and the Environment (IWÖ) is offering new certificate programme in
sustainability for bachelor students. Integrative Sustainability Management (SuM-HSG)
starting in fall 2024, provides students with the tools and skills to understand and
effectively tackle complex sustainability challenges. Students will learn to build
partnerships, make decisions, devise solutions, and implement initiatives essential to
driving progress towards a sustainable future. The SuM-HSG, granting 24 ECTS, is open for
students of all bachelor programmes (majors) after successful completion of the assessment
year, for no additional cost. 16 ECTS credits must be earned in courses that are open and
compulsory for SuM students only (Base Camp, 4 ECTS; Introduction to Sustainability
Management, 4 ECTS; Final Project, 8 ECTS). The remaining 8 ECTS come from
sustainability-focused electives, which students can select from approved courses across HSG
bachelor's programmes.

Find more information
**[here](https://www.unisg.ch/en/studying/programmes/bachelor/additional-qualifications/certificate-programme-integrative-sustainability-management-sum-hsg/)**
and feel warmly invited to inform your students about this exciting opportunity!

### Virtual Brownbag Seminar: Dr. Ann-Kristin Großkopf (LMU)

24 April 2024, 09:30–10:30 · online

Title: **Credible Environmental Commitments: Insights from Material Contracts**

Topic: This paper investigates whether the use of environmental contract clauses is a
signaling device for the credibility of firms' environmental pledges. With the rising
occurrence of extreme weather events and the growing public attention to climate change,
firms are experiencing mounting pressure from regulators and stakeholders to act in an
environmentally responsible manner. In response to this pressure, managers publicly pledge to
reduce their use of natural resources or cutting their CO2 emissions up to becoming net-zero
(Comello et al., 2021). However, while many of these environmental pledges are
well-intended, some firms use them for cheap-talk purposes, exploiting that the pledges are
not enforceable and often unverifiable for stakeholders. Consequently, stakeholders face
challenges in discerning genuine climate commitments from mere greenwashing (Desai et al.,
2023; Comello et al., 2023). To answer our research question, we first provide comprehensive
evidence on firms' use of environmental clauses before assessing the antecedents of using
such contract clauses. Building on these results, we explore whether firms deliberately use
environmental contract clauses as a signaling channel and whether stakeholders interpret such
clauses as a credible commitment.

### (in-person) Brownbag Lunch: Get your work published in Academy of Management Journal!

13 May 2024, 12:00–13:30 · HSG Square Arena

In this 60-minute brownbag talk (bring your own food),
**[Christian Tröster (KLU)](https://www.klu.org/faculty-research/faculty/resident-faculty/christian-troester)**
will share his experience as **Associate Editor at the *Academy of Management Journal*
(*AMJ*)**. He will discuss the publication process at *AMJ*, shed light on commonly
encountered weak features of research papers submitted to general management journals, and
elaborate on issues that potential authors should watch out for when articulating their
research insights and drafting their manuscripts for such outlets as *AMJ*. We will have
plenty of time for Q&A throughout as well as an informal coffee break together for the final
30min.

[Christian Tröster](https://www.klu.org/faculty-research/faculty/resident-faculty/christian-troester)
is a Professor of Leadership and Organizational Behavior at the Kühne Logistics University
(KLU) in Hamburg, Germany. Chris uses experiments and surveys to study topics in
organizational behavior, such as leadership, social networks, and teams. His work has been
published in *Academy of Management Journal*, *Journal of Applied Psychology*, *Journal of
International Business Studies*, *Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes*, etc.
Chris received his bachelor's and master's degrees in sociology from the University of
Groningen (NL) and his Ph.D. from the Rotterdam School of Management (Erasmus University
Rotterdam) (NL). He has been a visiting scholar at several universities, such as Singapore
Management University (SMU) and the University of British Columbia (UBC). He served on the
editorial board of *The Leadership Quarterly* and is currently associate editor of the
*Academy of Management Journal.*

### CSEAR Italy 2024 Conference - Call for papers: "Shaping the Future through Social and Environmental Accounting"

The Research Centre for Responsibility, Ethics and Sustainability in Management (RES.m HUB)
of the Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore (Italy), in collaboration with the CSEAR network,
is delighted to invite you to the 9th Italian Conference on Social and Environmental
Accounting Research (CSEAR Italy 2024).

This call for papers invites insights into modern corporations' evolving social and
environmental accounting challenges and accountability. Key topics include developing
effective sustainability metrics, integrating environmental and social considerations into
core business strategies, and balancing the diverse interests of stakeholders such as
shareholders, customers, employees, governments, and communities. Papers should address
navigating the complex regulatory landscape of ESG, ensuring transparency and ethical
practices in supply chains, and adapting operations to mitigate climate change impacts.

### Paper Development Workshop for a Special Issue of Journal of Business Ethics: Polycrisis & Precarity: Understanding the Lived Experience of Workers

The world in which businesspeople work is one of polycrisis: an unprecedented multitude of
cascading and interrelated challenges confront humanity and our planet. Amid polycrisis,
lived experience becomes likely to reflect the precarity that existentialist philosophers
have long considered the fundamental nature of the human condition.

We are calling for papers that take as their starting point the individual worker's
situatedness within polycrisis. The aim is to kickstart a stream of business ethics
scholarship that speaks to how moral agency can thrive despite the threats entailed in
polycrisis. We seek papers that draw from (1) existentialist and other philosophical
perspectives, (2) literary fiction and criticism, or (3) that use other conceptual and
empirical methods to understand individual workers' ways of coping with and confronting
polycrisis.

The full call for the Special Issue (deadline 1 May 2025) is here:
[https://link.springer.com/collections/hedibbgiab](https://link.springer.com/collections/hedibbgiab)
