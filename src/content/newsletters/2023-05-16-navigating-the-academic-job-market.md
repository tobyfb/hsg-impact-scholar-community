## Community events

### Navigating the academic job market for sustainability

30 May 2023, 15:15–16:45 · SQUARE, Säntis-Raum (11-2091), followed by an Apéro

**Why**: With this event we aim to support early-career academics in finding a job.

**Description**: In this session, we will discuss challenges, pitfalls, tips and resources
that are needed to successfully start an academic career in the field of sustainability.
Dr. Kerrigan Unter, postdoc at the Institute for Economy and the Environment (IWÖ) will
provide an input, followed by a panel discussion to share experiences of various researchers
and to discuss any questions that you may have. We will further exchange during an Apéro at
the end of the session.

We are delighted to announce the following panelists:

- *Dr. Miron Avidan, Assistant Professor for Sustainability and Business at Chatham
  University*
- *Prof. Merla Kubli, Assistant Professor for Managing Climate Solutions*
- *Prof. Stefano Ramelli, Assistant Professor in Corporate Finance*
- *Prof. Judith Ströhle, Assistant Professor of Sustainability Governance*
- *Moderator: Dr. Kerrigan Unter*

👉 Please **sign up**.

Looking forward to seeing many of you there!

Warm regards,

Tabea and Tobias for the HSG Impact Scholar Community 🤗
