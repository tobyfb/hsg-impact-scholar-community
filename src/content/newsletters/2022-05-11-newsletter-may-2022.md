## Looking back

### Community workshop hosted by the Chair for Sustainability Management

End of March, we had our first community workshop around the research topics at the chair for
sustainability management. The team of Prof. Judith Walls presented their research projects
including sustainability branding, sustainability strategies, circular economy, sustainable
leadership, sustainability and institutional complexity, business and biodiversity,
employee's green creativity, and trophy hunting. After the research pitches, we had a deep
dive into specific challenges and questions that each researcher is facing. It was a lively
and fruitful exchange.
