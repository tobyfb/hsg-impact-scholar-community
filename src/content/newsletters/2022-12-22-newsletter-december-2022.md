## Community events

### Academic Poster Session

1 March 2023, 15:30–17:20 · SQUARE

As part of the oikos Sustainability Week 2023, the Delegate's team for Responsibility and
Sustainability will organize an academic poster session to present current research projects
in the field of sustainability. We encourage PhD students, postdocs, and faculty members to
participate by presenting an academic poster about their research project. If you want to
participate with a poster, please **sign up by January 29th**.

The Delegate's team will sponsor the printing of the posters as well as drinks and snacks
during the event. If you want to participate without submitting a paper, please block the
date in your calendar - a registration link will be provided soon via oikos announcement.

## From the HSG ecosystem

### Workshop on Quantitative Sustainability Research

16 March 2023, full-day · SQUARE (tbc)

Prof. Judith Stroehle (IWÖ and ACA) and Prof. Florian Eugster (ACA) are organizing the 1st
workshop on Quantitative Sustainability Research. If you want to present your research,
**please submit your (theoretical or empirical) working paper by January 10th**. If you want
to participate without presenting a paper, please sign up with the organizers.

### ARCS 15th Annual Research Conference

5–8 June 2023 · University of Virginia

Since this year, the University of St. Gallen
[is an institutional member](https://www.unisg.ch/en/newsdetail/news/hsg-joins-alliance-for-research-on-corporate-sustainability-arcs/)
of the
[Alliance for Research on Corporate Sustainability (ARCS)](https://corporate-sustainability.org/),
a consortium of universities and individuals that serves as a professional society of
scholars studying the interface between business and sustainability. Their 15th annual
research conference will take place in summer next year. Please find more information
[here](https://corporate-sustainability.org/conferences/) and in their
[call for paper](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alliance-for-research-on-corporate-sustainability_call-for-papers-activity-7008094783084593152-z2mc/).
**Submission deadline is January 20th**.

### Water as a Human Right: Local solutions for global challenges

18 January 2023, 18:30 · SQUARE, 51-61 (followed by an apéro)

Since 2016, the University of St.Gallen is certified as a Blue University and member of the
Blue Community in Switzerland. Together with other actors of the Blue Community, the HSG is
organizing a public event with **UN special rapporteur Pedro Arrojo-Agudo**. Besides the
challenges of sustainable use of water, we will discuss global and local options for action
for different actors, e.g. cities and municipalities, federal government, research, students,
civil society, companies, and individuals.
