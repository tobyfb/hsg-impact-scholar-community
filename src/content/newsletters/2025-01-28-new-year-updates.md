Dear Impact Scholar

As we welcome the new year, we are looking forward to new opportunities to connect, exchange,
and co-create.

Below, we share three **upcoming events within the community** and further **information**
that might be interesting for you.

We are also looking forward to **co-creating events with you** for the next academic year, if
you have ideas you can email us at impact-scholar-community@unisg.ch.

Thank you and warm regards,
Eugenia, Tabea, Tobias and Alina for the HSG Impact Scholar Community

## Community events

### Impact Thesis - Event

3 March 2025, 16:00–17:30 · SQUARE Arena

The **HSG Impact Scholar Community** and **ECOnnect** are organizing an event to connect
students and scholars on impact research. Scholars can gain fresh perspectives and inspire
collaboration, while students can find guidance and support for writing impactful theses.

**What?** Scholars from different institutes and centers will showcase their research topics
highlighting opportunities to write thesis with them (5 minutes pitch per researcher, 1
Slide). The objective is to bring together students and scholars at HSG, united by a shared
interest in creating positive societal and environmental impact through research.

**Do you want to join?**

- **Register as a scholar to pitch your impact research to students** \[open until February
  28\]
- **Register as a student**

We envision this event as a two part series: Part 1 (March 3rd, 2025) aims to inspire students
and give them an overview of potential topics and supervisors for Bachelor's, Master's, or
Doctoral theses in the impact field at HSG. Part 2 will take place in fall 2025. During that
event, students who have finished or are finishing their thesis will be able to present their
key findings to the community.

We are looking forward to seeing many of you there!

### Academic Poster Session

3 March 2025, 09:30–11:30 · SQUARE Atrium

As part of the oikos sustainability week, HSG and OST are co-organising an Academic Poster
Session. Join us to present your research on the SDGs or a related topic, get inspired by
exchanging ideas with your colleagues, and find exciting opportunities for future research
collaborations!

**Step 1:** Indicate your interest to hand in a poster by February 12

**Step 2:** Submit the poster by February 24

**Step 3:** Attend the Academic Poster Sessions on March 3rd, 9:30 to 11:30 in the Atrium at
Square

You can contribute a poster on a recent publication or work in progress, even if you cannot
join the session in person. The poster should be in A0 (portrait), and include information on
the research title, background, methods, results, and contribution to theory & practice of
your project, as well as the author(s) name incl. contact details.

### Brownbag Lunch: What is impact research?

13 March 2025, 12:30–13:30, followed by informal exchange and networking · SQUARE, Personality in Residence Room (tbc)

On March 13th we will discuss Impact Research together with **Prof. Ed Freeman**, the father
of Stakeholder Theory and "icon" in the sustainability management field. After a fireside chat
with him, we will discuss in groups what impact research means for us, why impact is needed,
and how young scholars can have a positive impact with their research insights.

Lunch will be provided!

**What?** Brownbag Lunch

Please sign up.

## From the HSG ecosystem

### Positive Impact Collaboration

26 February 2025, 10:00–12:00 · SQUARE Arena

To create momentum towards a positive impact transformation at HSG, we need new participatory
infrastructures to develop our individual and shared capacities for collaboration and taking
effective action. The **Positive Impact Collaboration** aims to break down silos between
organizational boundaries and stakeholder groups to become aware of and learn from each other,
to scale existing positive impact and design new collaborative action.

Everyone at HSG working towards positive impact – whatever that means to them – is welcome to
join. We are committed to creating an environment of trust and open-mindedness where we
welcome and appreciate different perspectives and each other as full human beings.

Some snacks will be provided during the session. Lunch is not provided but for those who have
time we invite them to stay and connect over an informal lunch at the mensa.

### Mindful Co-working Days

This semester there will be more Mindful Coworking days at the Square! The idea is to share a
space where we can incorporate mindfulness while writing our thesis/papers. We combine formal
mindfulness practices with (silent) co-working time (3 hours in the morning and 3 hours in the
afternoon), and group exchanges. Next dates: 07/02, 14/03, 11/04

Feel free to share with friends who are writing their thesis (bachelor, master and PhDs are
welcome!).
