## Help rate HSG's material topics!

The **Working Group Social and Economic Sustainability Strategy** is currently in the process
of developing the social and economic aspects of an integrated sustainability strategy of the
University of St Gallen. See more context in the
[latest PRME Report 2021-2022](https://sustainability.unisg.ch/wp-content/uploads/University-of-St.Gallen_PRME_Report_2022_FINAL_WEB.pdf).

To meet its **mandate**, the Working Group engages internal and external stakeholders in
defining material topics by assessing the environmental, social and economic impacts of the
University in a process inspired by GRI.

And that's where **YOU** come into play!

The Working Group has narrowed its extensive research down to **13 material topics**.
According to the GRI, an organization's material topics are "**those topics that represent
its most significant impacts on the economy, environment, and people**". From this
perspective, we would like to ask you for 🫵 your opinion on...

- the **strategic relevance** of the topic on HSG's success
- the **overall impact** HSG currently has in the area of the topic
- the **trend** of the topic for the next 3-5 years

At the top of the form, you will find the link to a **topic glossary** with more detailed
information and a list of the 13 material topics.

After a quick read-through, you'll be able to fill out the **online survey** (5 minutes).

**Thank you** very much for your important insights and time – and for making the diverse
voices of HSG stakeholders heard! 🤝

If you have any questions or comments, please reach out to the Working Group.

### New sustainability data available for all HSG members

Carbon Disclosure Project (CDP) data is available for members of HSG! CDP has the most
