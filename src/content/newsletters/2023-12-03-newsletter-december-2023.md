### Research Session: Qualitative Research for Positive Impact

7 December 2023, 14:30–16:30 · SQUARE (Lounge 11-2042)

This session will provide insight into the process of conducting qualitative empirical
research on social sustainability topics. Perspectives on ethnography, interviews and case
studies will be presented, followed by a discussion and Q&A. The event is co-organized with
our community member Paula Bialski, Associate Professor of Digital Sociology, who will
present her research on "Hacking the Climate: researching climate activism online and off".

Please sign up. We're looking forward to exciting discussions!

## From the HSG ecosystem

### Male allyship training (Zurich or St.Gallen)

11 January 2024 (Zurich) or 18 January 2024 (St.Gallen)

"All hands on deck" are needed to make sustainable progress towards **gender
equity**—including men. But how to do this effectively? At the Competence Center for
Diversity and Inclusion (HSG), we've designed an **evidence-based**
[**male allyship training**](https://ccdi-unisg.ch/en/male-allyship-training/) exactly for
this purpose!

We invite all men who aim to learn and support gender equity at work to join our blended
learning approach of self-paced online content and a day on-site (In Zurich on January 11, in
St.Gallen on January 18).
[Prof. Dr. Jamie Gloor](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAb9nJEBzFaF-EHKUAPp4f6I79VnD1jIgxc),
a leading expert in the field and member of the Impact Scholar Community, will guide you on
your journey to allyship (funded by—and offered at a reduced rate—thanks to the Swiss
National Science Foundation).

**Ready to learn more?**
[Check out the CCDI website](https://ccdi-unisg.ch/en/male-allyship-training/) for more
information and spread the word about our training with your male colleagues, family members,
and friends!

### Mindful co-working day

15 December 2023 · SQUARE

We will be hosting another Mindful Coworking day at the Square. The idea is to share a space
where we can **incorporate mindfulness while writing our thesis/papers**. We combine formal
mindfulness practices with (silent) co-working time (3 hours in the morning and 3 hours in
the afternoon), and group exchanges.

Feel free to share with friends who are writing the thesis (bachelor, master and PhDs are
welcome!).

👉 If you are interested, please sign up.

### oikos / UN PRME LEAP Program 2024 application open

Call for LEAP researchers in 2024 organized by oikos and now in collaboration with UN PRME.
The program is aimed at Masters students, Ph.D. students, or postdoctoral researchers who
address sustainability challenges in their research and/or teaching and have a high
motivation to develop personally and professionally. Find more info
[here](https://www.unprme.org/news/prme-and-oikos-team-up-the-call-for-leap-researchers-2024-is-now-open/).
Quite a few community members have participated in this program. If you want to learn more,
just reach out!

### Impact Sabbatical Program For Swiss Researchers, Professors, and Lecturers At Swissnex in San Francisco

Swiss researchers, professors, and lecturers have the opportunity to spend an Impact
Sabbatical at Swissnex in San Francisco in 2024 (winter, spring and summer). Each researcher
gets individualized networking support by us to ensure high relevance to their work. We are
placing a special focus on sustainability and digital technologies/data/AI. For more
information reach out to community member Ann-Kristin Zobel, who shared this exciting
opportunity.
