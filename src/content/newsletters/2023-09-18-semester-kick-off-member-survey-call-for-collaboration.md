## Member survey 2023

We launched this community in the summer two years ago (see the
[initial invitation](/about/#how-it-started) and our
[first meeting](/events/2021-07-06-community-kick-off/)) in a team of 2 PhD students and 2
Post docs with institutional & moral support from various people (incl. Judith Walls & Dirk
Lehmkuhl). Over the course of the last two years, we have (co-)created around 15 events with
about 250 people attending in total and sent out around 20 newsletters to a list of about 80
community members.

We want to take this moment to get a better sense of what community members value about the
HSG Impact Scholar Community – and where they see opportunities for exciting new offerings
and directions. We would really appreciate if you took a few minutes to fill out the member
survey. (You could do it in 2min focusing on the quant questions – that's already great – but
we would be particularly interested in your answers to the more open questions.)

## Call for collaboration

Since the launch in July 2021,
[Felicitas](https://www.linkedin.com/in/felicitaspietrulla/) completed her PhD and went back
to work for McKinsey and
[Miron](https://www.chatham.edu/academics/graduate/mba/faculty/miron-avidan.html) became an
Assistant Professor of Business Sustainability at Chatham University. Tabea is still Manager
at Responsibility & Sustainability at HSG. Tobias framed the development of this community
into a part of the action research for his PhD – but now also must write his PhD. Long story
short, there will be less capacity from the original team to co-create events and newsletters
for the community over the next 6-12 months. With the continuous great feedback we are
getting from community members, we want to make sure that our lack of capacity does not
impede the flourishing of the community. We are, therefore, launching a **call for
collaboration**: if you are excited about what the community stands and aims for and could
imagine supporting and shaping the further development of the community, please reach out by
**sending us an email**!
