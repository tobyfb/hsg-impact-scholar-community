## Looking back

### Get together Impact Scholar Community

8 March 2022

During our last get together on March 8th, we illustrated our research and discussed our
work. It was a great way to get the conversation started and see links between each other's
work in a very short amount of time. We appreciated the in-person get-together and
discussions after a rather long time of just seeing people in Zoom Windows.

![Eleven hand-drawn sketches on sheets of paper laid out on a table, each illustrating a participant's research project](/media/newsletters/2022-03-22-newsletter-march-2022/research-illustrations.jpg)

### oikos sustainability week: Academic Poster Session and Sustainability Thesis Slam

On Monday last week, 37 posters were presented during the Academic Poster Session organized
and sponsored by the team of the delegate for Responsibility and Sustainability. Around 60
scholars and students joined the event and learned about the diversity of research projects
around sustainability at HSG. You can have a look at the posters at the Foyer of the main
building (1st floor) for the next two weeks.

At the Sustainability Thesis Slam, 14 institutes and centers pitched their research and
exchanged with students on possible thesis topics in different fields of sustainability, such
as finance, computer science, reporting, accounting, strategies, marketing, diversity,
inclusion, education and more. It was a great opportunity both for students and faculty to
connect with each other and get to know the broad range of research in sustainability.

The slides and posters will be shared on sustainability.unisg.ch, soon.

A huge thank you to the student team of oikos for their hard work in organizing an amazing
Sustainability Week!
