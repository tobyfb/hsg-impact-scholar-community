## Community events

### Conference Preparation Space (aka "Mock AOM")

25 July 2024, 15:15–17:00 · 36-u108

Quite a few of our community members got accepted for presentations at
[AOM 2024 in Chicago in August](https://aom.org/events/annual-meeting/future-annual-meetings/2024-innovating-for-the-future-policy-purpose-and-organizations).
For a few of us it's the first time being and presenting at AOM or other exciting
conferences. We will have a session that helps us in preparing and exchanging about
AOM/academic conferences more broadly. In this **"Mock AOM"** session attendees can test their
presentations and get feedback from peers and faculty. The idea is that this is not only a way
to prepare for our conference contributions individually but also an opportunity to get to
know other HSG people and their research. 🚀

Who: 1. Conference presenters, 2. First-time conference attendees 3. experienced presenters
(and everything in between)

Please sign up and indicate if you could participate with a mock/training presentation and/or
would be willing to provide feedback on presentations.

### Workshop: Fostering Creativity with Awareness through movement

We would like to organize a workshop on Awareness through movement for the Impact Scholar
Community. In the workshop (2h), we can learn to move with curiosity and joy, deepening
self-awareness and awareness of our surroundings. This can help us access our creative
potential.

If you are interested in joining this introductory workshop (no experience needed) you can let
us know.

### 8th St.Galler Diversity & Inclusion Week

16–20 September 2024

The 8th St. Galler Diversity & Inclusion Week will take place from 16 to 20 September 2024.
Numerous online sessions will explore the main theme, "Moving I Fatigue and Resistance," from
different perspectives. The Gender Intelligence Report 2024 will be launched in a hybrid
format on Tuesday afternoon. On Thursday, 19 September, the conference will take place in
St. Gallen, with a wide range of practical workshops, interesting inputs, and plenty of time
for networking.

The online programme is free of charge and all online sessions will be held in German and
English with simultaneous translation. There is a fee to attend the on-site conference.

See here: [Programme](https://inclusion-tagung.ch/en/programme/) |
[Registration](https://inclusion-tagung.ch/en/registration/)

### Call for proposals on climate finance by Norges Bank Investment Management

Call for proposals on climate finance by Norges Bank Investment Management: To develop and
advance the research efforts on climate finance, Norges Bank Investment Management welcomes
proposals from institutions and researchers that can address challenges such as: interactions
between climate, nature and financial risks, climate transition and geopolitics, climate
action and its effectiveness. More info:
[https://www.nbim.no/en/the-fund/investment-insights/call-for-proposals/call-for-proposals-for-climate-finance/](https://www.nbim.no/en/the-fund/investment-insights/call-for-proposals/call-for-proposals-for-climate-finance/)
