## Community events

### Impactful research in the context of Latin America: World Café during the Latin American Week

23 October 2024, 10:30–12:00 · SQUARE, Atrium

As part of the Latin American week, the HSG Impact Scholar Community is organizing a World
Café. If you are doing impact research related to Latin America, you can sign up to pitch your
research.

If you want to come as an audience, you can sign up for this session and many more here:
[https://latinamerica.unisg.ch/en/latin-america-week/anmeldung/](https://latinamerica.unisg.ch/en/latin-america-week/anmeldung/)

**What**:

- Colombian coffee degustation
- Discussion with social and environmental leaders of the Colombian Inga indigenous people
- World Café: Short research presentations and dialogue on doing sustainability research on
  Latin America with Prof. Dr. Thomas Grisaffi

### Join Our Monthly HSG Impact Scholar Research Meetings!

We are excited to announce the launch of our regular *online research meetings*, designed to
foster collaboration and knowledge-sharing within our community. Starting this month, we will
meet **on the last Friday of each month from 14:00 to 15:00** to discuss a variety of topics
related to our research and impact work.

Each meeting will feature a different topic, and you can visit our **Teams channel** to learn
about the upcoming discussion or propose new topics for future meetings. To keep things
simple, we will create a chat where the meetings will take place. For now, you can vote in
Teams on which topics you would like to discuss.

We look forward to connecting and exchanging ideas with all of you!

## From the HSG ecosystem

### Panel discussion: Advocating for Equity in STEM

5 November 2024, 17:00–19:30 · ETH Zurich (Main Building, HG E3)

At the upcoming Hello 50:50 World event "Advocating for Equity in STEM" in collaboration with
ETH Zürich (ETH Diversity) and University of St.Gallen (IRI), the expert panel will be
discussing this tricky balance between removing inequalities while also transforming existing
systems through allyship. Our community facilitator Eugenia will be on the panel of this
event!

**Format**: Panel discussion & networking apero, every participant brings a +1 with a
different perspective for a balanced conversation

[Advocating for Equity in STEM Tickets, Tue, Nov 5, 2024 at 5:00 PM | Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.ch/e/advocating-for-equity-in-stem-tickets-1024740094207?aff=oddtdtcreator)

### DocNet Symposium "Rethinking Growth: Perspectives on Social and Economic Prosperity"

21 November 2024, 16:00–21:00 · SQUARE, Discovery Grid

This event will challenge traditional growth models and explore new metrics for prosperity.
While acknowledging the benefits of growth, we will also reflect on its limits and the
socio-economic and environmental impacts. A key part of the discussion will focus on how
companies can embrace sustainability, leveraging innovative practices and technologies for
socially and environmentally responsible growth. We aim to foster dialogue on addressing
challenges like economic slowdown, resource inequality, and the need for sustainable corporate
strategies to create more inclusive, lasting growth.

More information and registration on the website:
[DocNet Symposium | DocNet (docnet-hsg.ch)](https://docnet-hsg.ch/docnet-symposium/)

### Workshop for young lecturers: Transforming our university teaching towards sustainability

23 October 2024, 13:00–17:00 · Waisenhausplatz 30, 3011 Bern

SDSN, WWF and other stakeholders active in academia, will organize a workshop on
sustainability in teaching. The goal of the workshop is to help young lecturers expand their
knowledge on the transformative potential of universities and how this change can be initiated
internally within universities. The workshop will provide essential basic knowledge, teaching
strategies, and support offers to incorporate sustainable development into teaching.

More information and registration:
[https://www.he-sustainability.ch/workshop2024](https://www.he-sustainability.ch/workshop2024)
