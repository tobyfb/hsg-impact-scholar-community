### Reminder: Job talk simulation by Dr. Miron Avidan on climate change disclosure

30 November 2022, 15:30–16:30

Join us for the job talk simulation by Dr. Miron Avidan. Please find the title and the
abstract below. Miron will give a 30 minutes presentation followed by critical Q&A from the
audience. It is a great opportunity for both the presenter and the audience to practice a job
talk situation and to get to know the format. Please **sign up**.

**Title:** TEMPORAL DYNAMICS IN CLIMATE CHANGE DISCLOSURE: THE INTERPLAY OF CONSISTENCY AND
ACCOUNTABILITY IN GREENHOUSE GAS TARGET SETTING

**Abstract:** Sustainability disclosure is a temporal process that builds on past commitments
toward the future. However, stakeholders discuss its quality solely based on a current
snapshot. To understand how sustainability disclosure changes with time, we tracked
Greenhouse (GHG) emissions reduction targets of 105 S&P companies across six sectors. Our
findings reveal a set of intra-firm temporal comparability issues. Across all industries,
more than 50% of the targets were either set in hindsight, shifted, or disbanded. We use
these findings to develop a typology of temporal comparability comprised of consistency and
accountability, thereby adding a temporal dimension to the greenwashing and impression
management literature. We provide recommendations for practitioners, policymakers, ESG
agencies, and the public on holding companies accountable for their past commitments.

### Call for interest: Action-oriented research exchange

Two members of the community, Clémentine Robert and Tobias Fehr-Bosshard, are pursuing their
PhD projects with an action research approach. Action research is a participatory approach
built around iterative loops of action and reflection. Being action-oriented, it aims to
simultaneously contribute to solving practical challenges and to creating scientific
knowledge. If there are other members of the community that are (or are considering) working
with similar action-oriented approaches, we would be very interested in an exchange and
discussions about developing a shared reflective practice. Please reach out to Clémentine and
Tobias and let's meet for coffee! ☕️

### ARCS membership / Call for Papers

In October, HSG joined the Alliance for Research on Corporate Sustainability (ARCS).
[ARCS](https://corporate-sustainability.org/) is a global partnership of top academic
institutions on sustainability research in management. Being an institutional member of ARCS
provides a great opportunity for HSG faculty to exchange with top researchers and gain access
to data in order to promote excellent sustainability research. ARCS holds a yearly conference
and other seminars and activities throughout the year. You can find all the
[events here](https://corporate-sustainability.org/events/). The next
[conference](https://corporate-sustainability.org/event/arcs-15th-annual-research-conference/)
will take place Jun 5-8, 2023 at the University of Virginia including a dedicated PhD
workshop. The Call for Papers (deadline January 10th, 2023) is
[here](https://files.constantcontact.com/61462dbb001/6c27620d-de51-469b-8d45-c6f0a00ba4ed.pdf).
Find more information about the ARCS membership
[here](https://www.unisg.ch/en/wissen/newsroom/aktuell/rssnews/campus/2022/oktober/hsg-joins-arcs-20oktober2022).
