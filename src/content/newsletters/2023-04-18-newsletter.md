### ERS Reflection Session (Prototype)

16 May 2023, 16:00–17:30

Some of the community members participated in the course
[Ethics, Responsibility & Sustainability (ERS) in Research and the PhD Journey](https://tools.unisg.ch/handlers/Public/CourseInformationSheet.ashx/semester/HS22/eventnumber/10,161,1.00.pdf)
last semester. One exercise we found quite meaningful was an ERS reflection session around
the following questions:

1. *Where do I come from? How did I end up here? What drives me?*
2. *Why did I pick my PhD topic?*
3. *Which ERS topics are relevant for my research?*
4. *How can I make an impact in practice?*
5. *What do I need from my peers?*
6. *How do I position myself within my field of research?*

In small groups of 3-5 people there was a short but deep conversation around these questions
for 10-15 minutes for each person.

We thought that these kinds of ERS reflection sessions might be an interesting practice to
bring to the community.

We are organizing a prototype at the end of April and are interested in your feedback on
whether you would be interested in additional, regular sessions once or twice a semester.

Please sign up. If you have questions or comments about the format, please reach out.

## From the HSG ecosystem

### HSG Ecosystem Navigator

A collaboration between the Competence Center for Social Innovation (CSI-HSG) and
oikos/[ChangeHub](http://changehub.ch/) recently launched a
**[HSG Ecosystem Navigator](https://hsg.ecosystem-navigator.org/)**. Its goal is to make
visible the people, organizations, and the broad range of initiatives and events at HSG
related to sustainability and creating a positive impact and to reveal potential for
collaboration across disciplines and domains. Please find additional information
[HERE](https://hsg.ecosystem-navigator.org/initiatives/%F0%9F%9A%80+Academic+poster+-+Facilitating+impact-focused+collaboration+at+HSG+with+the+Ecosystem+Navigator)
and a prototype of the Ecosystem Navigator [HERE](https://hsg.ecosystem-navigator.org/). We
hope to leverage the HSG Ecosystem Navigator more for the HSG Impact Scholar Community in the
future. Feel free to add your 👤🏢🚀 profile, organization, initiative, 🗓 event or 💡idea for
impactful future action. Please reach out if you have any questions or comments.

### ONE-SIM Outreach Award

The Organizations and the Natural Environment (ONE) and Social Issues in Management (SIM)
divisions of the Academy of Management (AOM) issue the Outreach Award, which honours
researchers who have published a paper on ONE and SIM issues and have effectively
communicated the insights of their paper to broader, non-academic audiences. You can apply by
end of April
[HERE](https://omt.aom.org/communities/community-home/digestviewer/viewthread?MessageKey=c6d7cfa6-89ef-43fe-bd0e-25e2b6ae45f0&CommunityKey=3721a7ab-b230-4bfc-917c-e95b1a27b4db&bm=c6d7cfa6-89ef-43fe-bd0e-25e2b6ae45f0#bmc6d7cfa6-89ef-43fe-bd0e-25e2b6ae45f0).
