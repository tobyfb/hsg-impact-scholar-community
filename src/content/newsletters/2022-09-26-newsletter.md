### Job talk simulation by Dr. Miron Avidan

26 October 2022, 15:30

Join us for the job talk simulation by Dr. Miron Avidan. Please find the title and the
abstract below. Miron will give a 30 minutes presentation followed by critical Q&A from the
audience. It is a great opportunity for both the presenter and the audience to practice a job
talk situation and to get to know the format. Please **sign up**.

**Title:** TEMPORAL DYNAMICS IN CLIMATE CHANGE DISCLOSURE: THE INTERPLAY OF CONSISTENCY AND
ACCOUNTABILITY IN GREENHOUSE GAS TARGET SETTING

**Abstract:** Sustainability disclosure is a temporal process that builds on past commitments
toward the future. However, stakeholders discuss its quality solely based on a current
snapshot. To understand how sustainability disclosure changes with time, we tracked
Greenhouse (GHG) emissions reduction targets of 105 S&P companies across six sectors. Our
findings reveal a set of intra-firm temporal comparability issues. Across all industries,
more than 50% of the targets were either set in hindsight, shifted, or disbanded. We use
these findings to develop a typology of temporal comparability comprised of consistency and
accountability, thereby adding a temporal dimension to the greenwashing and impression
management literature. We provide recommendations for practitioners, policymakers, ESG
agencies, and the public on holding companies accountable for their past commitments.

### Forschen Universitäten zum Nutzen der Gesellschaft? Ein Workshop zur Messung des societal Impacts von Wissenschaft

22 November 2022, 13:30–19:00 · Universität St.Gallen (HSG)

In den letzten Jahrzehnten wurde eine Reihe von Instrumenten und Methoden zur Evaluation des
so genannten societal Impact von Wissenschaft entwickelt. Die Messung der spezifischen
Relevanz von Forschung für die Gesellschaft, z. B. im Hinblick auf die United Nations
Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), stellt die empirische Wissenschaftsforschung nicht nur
vor konzeptionelle, sondern auch vor methodische Herausforderungen. Diese Herausforderungen
haben wir zum Anlass genommen, einen Workshop mit Vorträgen und Diskussionen hierzu zu
organisieren. **Themenschwerpunkte:**

- Societal Impact von Forschung
- Daten für die Messung von societal Impact (z. B. anhand der Sustainable Development Goals,
  SDGs)
- Bedeutung der societal Impact Messung für die Evaluation von Forschungsleistungen an
  Universitäten
- Konzeptioneller Rahmen für die Verwendung von Indikatoren in der Forschungsevaluation.

**Format:** Impulsvorträge, Diskussionen, Plenumdiskussion von/mit Expertinnen/Experten und
Wissenschaftlerinnen/Wissenschaftlern (Max Planck Society, Fraunhofer Institute for Systems
and Innovation Research ISI, ETH Zurich Library, HEC Lausanne - University of Lausanne,
Center for Higher Education and Science Studies - University of Zurich; University of
St.Gallen - Institute for Economy and the Environment, Institute for Systemic Management and
Public Governance)

**Zielgruppe** sind Forschende und Interessierte an Szientometrie, Bibliometrie, Altmetrie
und Forschungsevaluation, Universität St.Gallen, Universität Zürich, ETH Zürich.

**Teilnehmendenzahl: Max. 40 Personen**

**Ort:** Universität St.Gallen (HSG), 9000 St.Gallen *(Detailed information follows)*

### Curriculum Development Sustainability

HSG is developing tools and programs to better embed sustainability into the curriculums
across levels and programs. As lecturers or TAs, benefit from them this semester!

**Sustainability TAP: A new tool to support you better embed sustainability into your
course**

Assess your course in a qualitative way with the support of the students.

Sustainability Teaching Analysis Polls are specially designed for lecturers to get the
support they need from their students to collaboratively improve the integration of
sustainability in their courses - regardless of the topic, discipline or program. If you are
a TA feel free to suggest this tool to the professor.

Sustainability TAPs follow the overall structure and procedures of the standard TAP, while
additionally offering the opportunities:

- for students to share their sustainability-related learning experiences in a specific
  course in order to improve the integration of sustainability themes in that course. This
  integration, in turn, helps equip students to face existing and future sustainability
  challenges related to the course topic and discipline.
- for lecturers to gain insights on sustainability from their students via an anonymous
  qualitative method conducted by the TAP Team. Lecturers get customized input to keep
  improving the content and implementation of sustainability-related course topics.

There are only benefits, so do not hesitate and register for a Sustainability TAP for your
course.

**HDZ Seminar on Fostering Responsibility & Sustainability in Student Learning: Develop &
rethink your course with the support of experts in didactics and sustainability**

Looking at rethinking your teaching in the context of Responsibility and Sustainability? This
semester, join the HDZ Seminar on Fostering Responsibility & Sustainability in Student
Learning!

After running a successful pilot last semester, the
[Hochschuldidaktisches Zentrum](https://hdz.unisg.ch/de) (HDZ) and the
[Delegates Team for Responsibility and Sustainability](https://sustainability.unisg.ch/)
(R&S) join forces once more to provide support to whoever is interested (from any
discipline/field) in exploring ways to foster responsibility and sustainability into student
learning.

In this course you will:

- *Understand* R&S as a driver for teaching innovation at HSG.
- *Get inspired* by other participants' experiences, as well as by methodological and
  sustainability-related content resources.
- *Exchange* with peers and experts on your challenges in designing a constructive and
  innovative learning environment integrating R&S (submit questions / find solutions to
  hindrances).
- *Rethink and innovate* your current or future course and find the support you need to
  integrate R&S into it.

Date: 14.11.2022, 09:00-11:00 (online) & 23.11.2022, 12:00-16:00 (onsite)

More information and registration:
[here](https://hdz.unisg.ch/de/angebot/aktuelles-weiterbildungsprogramm/2022/2022_sustainability)
