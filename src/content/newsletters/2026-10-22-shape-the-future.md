## Community news

### We are looking for you!

As co-founders, we have been thinking about the **next phase of the community**: Where should
it develop in the future? Which topics, formats, and connections do current PhD students
need? And what does it take to stay relevant and active? We are convinced that the best
people to answer these questions are those who are in the middle of the PhD journey right
now. 🙌

Our own PhD time concluded a while ago, so we would love to hand over active stewardship to
new people, ideally current PhD students, who want to co-shape the future of the community.

*What would this look like?*

- Co-organize at least one main community event per year
- Keep the community alive with regular updates on relevant developments
- Everything else is up to you, based on your capacity and drive. Bring your own ideas! 💡

*What you get out of it:*
Being active in the community is a great way to shape your own PhD journey. You build your
network across disciplines, get in touch with experts at HSG and beyond, gain visibility for
your ideas, and develop new skills along the way.

Interested or just curious? Reach out to Tabea and Tobias directly, without any obligation.
Or let's chat at the Community Get-Together end of November. 🤩

## From the HSG ecosystem

### HSG Sustainability Report 2025

Did you know that more than half of the ventures awarded with the HSG Startup or Spinoff
Label in 2025 focus their business activities on solving sustainability challenges? Or that
the amount of solar power generated on the HSG campus last year corresponds to the annual
consumption of around 175 average Swiss two-person households? 🌻

Discover these and more facts in the 👉
[**HSG Sustainability Report 2025**](https://www.unisg.ch/de/universitaet/engagement/verantwortung-und-nachhaltigkeit/nachhaltigkeitsbericht/)!

Over the past weeks, the HSG Sustainability Services team updated the data and examples
across all areas of HSG's sustainability strategy:

- 👩‍🏫 Teaching
- 📝 Research
- 🤝 Societal Engagement
- 🏛️ Campus & Operations

The updated report continues to show how sustainability is embedded holistically in HSG's
activities. It also highlights how sustainability contributes to HSG's strategic goals of
excellence, talent development, and innovation.

Do you have an idea how to further improve sustainability at HSG? Or would like to
collaborate? The HSG Sustainability Services team is looking forward to hearing from you.

### Zukunftsfestival

![Open Space at the Prologue of the Zukunftsfestival St.Gallen: about twenty-five people sitting in a circle in a workshop room](/media/newsletters/2026-10-22-shape-the-future/zukunftsfestival-open-space.jpg)

On 18 and 19 September, a Prologue to the **Zukunftsfestival St.Gallen** invited the city to
develop a culture of collaboration and future-making to shape a desirable future for the
St.Gallen region. Over the two days, and a
[diverse program](https://zukunftsfestival.ch/programm), around 2,000 people became aware of
the festival, more than 300 contributed directly, and more than 30 organizations discussed
how to weave their activities together and build shared momentum.

Highlights included a **Bridge Lab** by CLIS-HSG, in which participants from different
sectors helped Hilti and Spare Energy turn ideas on the second life of batteries into
business models, and the AI-supported Albert(ine) persona at OST, which collected visitors'
challenges and wishes for the future, as well as an **Open Space** with partners. Find more
impressions in [this LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tobias-fehr-bosshard-6a6b8a22_am-vergangenen-freitag-und-samstag-fand-der-ugcPost-7508127310588416001-elJ1/).

The Prologue was initiated by the
[St.Gallen Transformation Lab](https://transformation-lab.co/) (HSG and OST), supported by
the City of St.Gallen and "Gemeinsam wirkt", and carried by a growing network of partners; if
you would like to help shape what comes next, get in touch! A harvesting and planning for
2027 event will take place on **October 30th**.

### ENGAGE.EU Joint Master programme "Global Sustainability Management"

The Global Sustainability Management (GSM) Master programme is a career-shaping international
experience. GSM is co-developed and co-taught by experts from leading European institutions
on Business, Economics and Social Sciences. GSM prepares students with the sustainability
approaches engrained deep in today's business world.
