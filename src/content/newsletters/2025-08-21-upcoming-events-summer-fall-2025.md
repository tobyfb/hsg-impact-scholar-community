## Community events

### An introduction to the Ownership Project with Nien-hê Hsieh, HBS

27 August 2025, 14:00 · onsite, Müller-Friedbergstrasse 6/8, Floor 7, 52-7024

[Nien-hê Hsieh](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/profile.aspx?facId=24284) is the Kim B.
Clark Professor of Business Administration in the General Management Unit at Harvard Business
School. His research and teaching aims at helping business leaders and organizations determine
and deliver on their responsibilities. He is co-supervising the PhD of Catherine McDonald at
the Institute of Business Ethics (IWE-HSG) and will be visiting St.Gallen for her PhD defense.
Following the defense earlier in the morning, IWE-HSG is co-organizing an event with the HSG
Impact Scholar Community. Nien-hê Hsieh will provide an introduction to the
[Ownership Project](https://www.hbs.edu/bigs/about/research/the-ownership-project) followed by
a discussion with attendees.

Small apéro included. 🥂

Please sign up.

### Workshop: Who Gets Credit? Addressing Authorship Challenges in Academic Research

24 September 2025, 15:00–17:00 · onsite, 58-424

Regardless on which field you are in, authorship decisions – who is named and in which order –
will have major implications for our academic careers, but these decisions are often made in
opaque and inconsistent ways. Power dynamics, disciplinary norms, and unclear expectations can
lead to misunderstandings, authorship conflict, and even potentially harm to early-career
researchers.

In this interactive workshop with
[Dr. Mary Hausfeld](https://www.business.uzh.ch/en/research/professorships/hrmlead/Team/Staff/Mary-Hausfeld.html),
we'll explore the challenges and complexities of authorship and academic collaboration,
focusing on fairness, transparency, and navigating complex power dynamics.

There will be an apéro following the event (see more below).

Please sign up (Workshop + Apéro).

### Community Semester Kick-off: Celebrating achievements, setting intentions

24 September 2025, 17:00

Following the authoring event, we plan to not only have a nice apéro but also (re-)connect by
sharing a bit about personal developments and achievements of the previous academic year and
setting intentions for what is to come. We do this in an optional and joyful way. Feel free to
bring an artifact to share (e.g. your printed out dissertation or article, a picture from your
workshop / presentation at AOM, EGOS, ... or something similar) to get conversations started.

There will also be an opportunity to help co-create the future of the Impact Scholar
Community.

If you can only join the community kick-off starting at 17:00, please sign up separately (*no
need to sign up again if you already signed up for the authoring event!*)

### Business for Humanity with Tim Jackson – The Care Economy

3 September 2025, 17:00 CEST · online

Join us for an inspiring conversation with **Tim Jackson**, one of the world's leading
ecological economists and a powerful voice for reimagining prosperity beyond growth. Professor
of Sustainable Development at the University of Surrey and Director of the Centre for the
Understanding of Sustainable Prosperity (CUSP), Tim has spent over three decades challenging
the belief that progress must be measured by ever-increasing GDP.

In this session, Tim will introduce his vision for *The Care Economy*—an economic paradigm
that puts the health of people, communities, and the planet at the very heart of prosperity.
We will explore how shifting from a "careless economy" to a care-centered one can create the
foundations for a thriving, fair, and sustainable future.

Get your personal Zoom link by registering.

Don't miss this opportunity to engage with one of the most influential thinkers shaping the
future of economics and society.
