## Community events

### Impact Thesis - Event

3 March 2025, 16:00–17:30 · SQUARE Arena

The **HSG Impact Scholar Community** and **ECOnnect** are organizing an event to connect
students and scholars on impact research. Scholars can find support for research projects,
while students can get guidance for writing impactful theses.

**What?** Scholars from different institutes and centers will showcase their research topics
(5 minutes pitch per researcher, 1 Slide). The objective is to bring together students and
scholars at HSG, united by a shared interest in creating positive societal and environmental
impact through research.

**Do you want to join?**

- **Register as a scholar to present your impact research to students** \[open until February
  28\]
- **Register as a student**

We envision this event as a two part series: Part 1 (March 3rd, 2025) aims to inspire students
and give them an overview of potential topics and supervisors for Bachelor's, Master's, or
Doctoral theses in the impact field at HSG. Part 2 will take place in fall 2025. During that
event, students who have finished or are finishing their thesis will be able to present their
key findings to the community.

### Brownbag Lunch: What is impact research? A discussion with Prof. Ed Freeman

13 March 2025, 12:30–13:30, followed by a networking lunch · SQUARE, Residency Grid 11-0131

Join us for an inspiring discussion on impact research with the world-renowned expert Prof.
Edward Freeman, led by Prof. Julian Kölbel. Together, we will explore what impact truly means
and how you can contribute through your research.

This event is open to students, faculty, PhDs, and anyone passionate about impact research.
Don't miss this opportunity to discover how to create positive change through research,
connect with like-minded people and exchange ideas that drive meaningful impact.

Lunch will be provided.

**What?** Brownbag lunch combined with a fireside chat

Please sign up.

## From the HSG ecosystem

### Educating for Responsible Leadership in Business and Society with Prof. Ed Freeman

11 March 2025, 15:30–17:30 · SQUARE, Residency Grid 11-0131

**What can we do in the classroom to build competencies for responsible leadership in our
students?**

Join the internationally renowned Prof. Ed Freeman, a pioneer in stakeholder theory, for an

Hosted by the HSG Sustainability Curriculum Development Team, this workshop is designed for

Don't miss this opportunity to enhance your teaching and/or programs as part of empowering HSG

### The Future of Business: Responsible Business Without Trade-Offs? A discussion with Prof. Ed Freeman

12 March 2025, 16:15–17:30 + Apéro · SQUARE, Arena 11-2091

**Discussing Stakeholder Capitalism - A fig leaf or a real solution?**

What is the role of business in a world of increasing social erosion and accelerated
environmental degradation? Is generating profit for shareholders the only nature of a
capitalism? Join us for an engaging workshop with Prof. Ed Freeman, exploring if and how
businesses can build synergetic relations with people, planet and profit.

### Research talk by Jan Schmutz: Agile Teams and the Boundary Conditions of Team Reflexivity on Burnout and Innovation

24 February 2025, 11:30–12:30 · SQUARE, Lounge 11-2042

Jan B. Schmutz is an SNF professor at the Department of Psychology at the University of
Zurich, Switzerland. He received his Ph.D. from the University of Fribourg, Switzerland. His
research examines how team processes and emergent states (e.g., psychological safety, team
cohesion) influence individual and team outcomes. A specific focus of his research is on how
teams reflect and adapt when facing extreme events. Therefore, he collaborates with a number
of healthcare institutions in Europe, various Polar Programs, and the European Space Agency.

Join us for a lunch talk (feel free to bring a sandwich or something to snack on during his
presentation).

### 2025 PRME Education Awards: Applications are open!

The Principles for Responsible Management Education (PRME) is excited to announce the PRME
Education Awards, recognizing excellence in implementing sustainable development and
responsible management practices in business education. Awards will go to recognize excellence
in integration of the [Principles of PRME](https://www.unprme.org/what-we-do/), including in
the areas of Teaching, Research, Partnership, Practice, Values, and Purpose. The PRME
Education Awards seek to honor innovative and impactful contributions that advance the
Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and align with the educational interests of the PRME
community. We believe that by acknowledging and rewarding outstanding interventions, we can
drive meaningful change in transforming business education.

👉 [2025 PRME Education Awards: Applications are open! | UNPRME](https://www.unprme.org/news/2025-prme-education-awards-applications-are-open/)
