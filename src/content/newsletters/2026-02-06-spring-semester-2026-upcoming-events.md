### Academic Poster Session

16 April 2026, 13:00–15:00 · SQUARE Atrium

Together with the Sustainability Services team, we are organizing the **fifth edition** of the
Academic Poster Session at SQUARE.

The Academic Poster Session provides a platform to present **current research projects** on
topics around ethics, responsibility, sustainability and impact scholarship in general. We
like to encourage PhD students, Postdocs, and faculty members to participate by presenting an
academic poster about a recent publication or a work in progress project, even if they cannot
attend the session in person.

This year, we are also opening up a second track to present posters on **innovative teaching
formats** to create more visibility and awareness for that important impact pathway.

The costs of printing the posters will be covered. Drinks and snacks will be provided during
the event. After the session, the posters will be displayed in the main building for two weeks
(opt-out option). The exhibition will be open to all students and employees of the University
of St.Gallen as well as to the interested public.

👉 Please sign up

🙌 *Thank you, Julian Bömelburg, for once again leading the organization of this session*

### Exploring the Impact of Your Research – together

*Virtual event series and reflective space for HSG impact scholars*

In Spring 2026, the HSG Impact Scholar Community connects with the global
[AOM Impact Scholar Community](https://www.impactscholarcommunity.com/) for dynamic virtual
sessions designed to help you foster meaningful connections, professional development, and a
deeper understanding of research impact by addressing various topics.

Each session features a live panel with internationally renowned researchers, followed by a
short interactive Q&A. Afterwards, **HSG participants join a dedicated Zoom space to dive
deeper into the topic**, exchange experiences, reflect on challenges, and support one another
in exploring how to address the topic in their own work.

Whether you are just starting out or already an engaged scholar, this series offers
inspiration, practical insights, and a supportive community to help you turn research into
real-world impact.

Dates & topics:

- February 13th 4pm-5.30pm **'Engage with the Public: Connect with Communities that benefit
  from your research'** // Researchers interested in meaningfully engage with the public
  through public communications. Guests are Andrew Crane (University of Bath), Carmen Quinonez
  (University of Michigan), and HSG Impact Scholar Community member Jamie Gloor 🙌 (University
  of St.Gallen).
- April 17th 5.30pm-7pm **'Teach for Impact: Leverage pedagogy and mentorship to drive local
  change'** // Researchers interested in impactful teaching and mentorship. Guests are Kam
  Phung (Simon Fraser University), Kate Odziemkowska (University of Toronto), and Steve
  Kennedy (Erasmus University).
- June 11th 4pm-5.30pm **'Shape the Future: Identify emerging challenges and address them
  through responsive research'** // Researchers interested in addressing emerging and evolving
  societal issues. Guests are Ali Aslan Gümüsay (University of Munich), Grace Augustine
  (University of Bath), and Oana Branzei (Western University).

👉 If you are interested in participating in one or multiple sessions please sign up to
receive a calendar invite and Zoom link

🙌 *Thank you, Clémentine Robert, for creating this space for community members!*

## From the HSG ecosystem

### Business & Biodiversity Conference in relation to O&E special issue

20–21 March 2026 · SQUARE

The newly upgraded Institute of Responsible Innovation, Sustainability and Energy (HSG-RISE)
will host a 2-day conference on **business and biodiversity** linked to the special issue in
[Organization & Environment](https://www.linkedin.com/company/organizationandenvironment/).

The keynote, paper sessions, and the early-career workshop will follow a hybrid format –
guests are welcome to register for online attendance.

Applications are now open for the **Early-Career Workshop**, where PhD students, post-docs,
and early-career professors can exchange ideas and receive feedback on their research.

🚨 *Deadline for submissions: February 15*, more info
[**HERE**](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/judithwalls_business-biodiversity-management-activity-7417594482549944320-GETu)

### Positive Impact Community (PIC) Meet-ups 2026
