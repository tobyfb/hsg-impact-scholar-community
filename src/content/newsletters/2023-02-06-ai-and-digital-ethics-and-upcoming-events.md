Dear members of the HSG Impact Scholar Community,

We are happy to inform you that our community is the **first local spin-off** of the
[Global Impact Scholar Community](https://one.aom.org/one/working-groups/impact-scholar-community),
which is supported by the Academy of Management (AOM) and hosted by the Organization and the
Natural Environment (ONE) division. From the beginning on, our local community was inspired
by the Impact Scholar Community of the AOM-ONE and we are happy to be connected to this
inspiring global network of top scholars. You are welcome to join their events and if you
have ideas for co-creation, please reach out to us.

We would like to draw your attention to our **next community event**, co-hosted by the
**Institute for Business Ethics (IWE-HSG)**:

## Community events

### "AI promises and perils: A discussion of different perspectives of digital ethics"

15 February 2023, 15:00–17:00 · SQUARE, Hilti Innovation Room 1, followed by an apéro

**Format**: This event will discuss the challenges associated with AI in society and
approaches to offer potential solutions and understanding enabled by perspectives from
digital ethics and human rights. Team members of the Institute of Business Ethics (IWE-HSG)
will provide short inputs as basis for discussions in a world café format. Please bring your
own research, interests, and perspectives to the discussions.

Inputs include:

- [Florian Krause](https://iwe.unisg.ch/en/personenverzeichnis/70e14822-a48b-456c-b6ad-6cce87f67249)
  on the ethical challenges of metaphors in the context of AI
- [Ingrid Becker](https://iwe.unisg.ch/en/personenverzeichnis/6437e632-182d-4337-9960-305389ad794b)
  on opportunity and trust issues related to AI and blockchain
- [Kebene Wodajo](https://iwe.unisg.ch/en/personenverzeichnis/50cc6930-fdf3-4b51-9adb-96793e1dff76)
  on structural injustice in the context of AI
- [Mariam Shakil](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mariam-shakil18/) on technological solutionism
- [Thorsten Busch](https://iwe.unisg.ch/en/personenverzeichnis/58bc0fdb-94cf-488e-bd98-b4c2515ef3fb)
  on human perception and the hype around AI

**Background and context**: AI is said to have the potential to contribute to solving some of
the world's grand challenges. Yet, concerns about the conditions and actual adverse impacts
of AI on society and the planet are emerging. Some of these concerns include data bias and
gender data gaps, structural changes in the job market leading to worker redundancies, job
displacement, privacy concerns, issues surrounding fair, transparent and trustworthy AI
techniques, accountability, transparency, and the potential for misuse and exploitation for
nefarious purposes, such as gender-based violence or overarching surveillance practices,
function creep and deepening of existing inequalities.

We are looking forward to seeing many of you there!

All the best and warm regards,

Tabea and Tobias for the HSG Impact Scholar Community

### Extended deadline: Academic Poster Session

1 March 2023, 15:30–17:20 · SQUARE

As part of the oikos Sustainability Week 2023, the Delegate's team for Responsibility and
Sustainability will organize an academic poster session to present current research projects
in the field of sustainability. We already have great participants and there are a few spots
left. Therefore, we **extended the sign up period by February 9th**. We encourage PhD
students, postdocs, and faculty members to participate by presenting an academic poster about
their research project.

The Delegate's team will sponsor the printing of the posters as well as drinks and snacks
during the event. If you want to participate without submitting a paper, please block the
date in your calendar - a registration link will be provided soon via oikos announcement.
