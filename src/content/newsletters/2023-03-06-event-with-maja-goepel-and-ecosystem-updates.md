Dear members of the HSG Impact Scholar Community,

we hope that you had a great start into the new week!

During the last weeks, we had two inspiring events – the community event hosted by the
Institute for Business Ethics (IWE-HSG) on AI & digital ethics as well as the academic poster
session with 37 posters on current sustainability-related research (see LinkedIn post
[here](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sustainability-at-hsg-and-econnect_last-wednesday-the-team-for-responsibility-activity-7038419651046821888-cqJK)).
It was fantastic to connect with so many of you and to discuss your research projects. Thank
you for contributing!

We already have the next exciting event lined-up: We have the great honour to host a workshop
with Prof. Maja Göpel, a famous and influential transformation researcher and sustainability
expert. She will join HSG as a Personality in Residence at SQUARE.

## Community events

### "HSG 2030: What insight? What impact? And how do we get there?"

13 March 2023, 15:00–17:00 · SQUARE, Wintergarten (1st floor), followed by an apéro

**Purpose and outcomes**: We are currently facing multiple ecological and social crises.
Business schools influence our thinking and how our socio-economic systems function. They can
play an important role in tackling grand challenges through research, teaching and
collaboration. We want to discuss what HSG specifically and concretely could contribute in
this "Decade of Action".

Under the title "HSG 2030: What insight? What impact? And how do we get there?" there will be
short vision statements of a more impactful future by different stakeholders in the HSG
ecosystem. Prof. Maja Göpel will provide a crucial additional outside-in perspective.
Inspired by these outlooks, we will conduct a world café to co-create and discuss actionable
ideas for further developing and scaling HSG's positive impact.

We are looking forward to seeing many of you there!

All the best and warm regards,

Tabea and Tobias for the HSG Impact Scholar Community

### GCE&I Research Talk with Prof. Dr. Jukka-Pekka Heikkilä on "Entrepreneurship education in extreme settings and situations"

21 March 2023, 11:00–12:30 · Room A 24-0-130 and online

Jukka-Pekka recently published a paper in Academy of Management Discoveries about
"[Envisioning Entrepreneurial Engagement in North Korea](https://journals.aom.org/doi/full/10.5465/amd.2020.0066)"
and he will also share his experience of the publication process with us. I find this paper
very interesting as it's one of those newer types of articles where the "researcher's voice
and experience" are at the center and used as data. Sign up via GCE&I.
