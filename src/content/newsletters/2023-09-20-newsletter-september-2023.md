## Offers for faculty and students to integrate sustainability into teaching

### Sustainability Teaching Analysis Poll

- For faculty: Get your student feedback on how sustainability is integrated into your
  course!
- For PhD students: Encourage your faculty to get your student feedback on how sustainability
  is integrated into their course!

Faculty can request to get students qualitative feedback by registering to a **Sustainability
Teaching Analysis Poll**.

### Slide deck reviewed by students

Are you interested in getting your **slide deck reviewed by students** so that they can
support you in further integrating sustainability into your course?

Reach out to be put in touch with highly motivated students ready to provide you with
constructive suggestions on how/where to further integrate sustainability into your course.
Exchange together to make the final decisions on what suggestions are accepted and
implemented.

### Sustainability Curriculum Innovation Lab

If you are a faculty: Join the **Sustainability Curriculum Innovation Lab** session.

If you are a PhD student: Help oikos curriculum change team to get in touch with a faculty to
take part in the Sustainability Curriculum Innovation Lab session. As a student, you can also
join yourself the upcoming session (tbc).

In a workshop format, students brainstorm on how to further integrate sustainability in the
course presented by the faculty, according to its learning objective and diverse constraints.
The goal: the faculty leaves with concrete and right away implementable ideas!
[Sustainability Curriculum Innovation Lab (SCIL) - Responsibility & Sustainability (unisg.ch)](https://sustainability.unisg.ch/news/sustainability-curriculum-innovation-lab-2023/)

### The impactful 5 Playbook, PRME (Transforming Business Education through Creative Pedagogies)

With the Impactful Five (i5) project, the United Nations Principles for Responsible
Management Education (PRME) ambition is to foster a new generation of students with the
skills needed to address the global challenges of our time, address the Sustainable
Development Goals (SDGs), and impact generations of educators and students with a new
paradigm for creative pedagogy.

The i5 project challenges 'mainstream' teaching and learning practices to infuse meaning,
joy, and 'soft skills' into education. Through a partnership with Project Zero at the Harvard
Graduate School of Education, PRME analyzed research from The LEGO Foundation childhood and
youth development research-spheres to create a pedagogy for the higher education business
school context. These findings were translated into a Playbook for educators from the PRME
community to utilize as a tool in their classroom and to better engage with the i5
characteristics of making learning meaningful, joyful, actively engaging, social, and
iterative.
