## Looking back

### Impact Research Event with Prof. Edward Freeman

On March 13, the HSG Impact Scholar Community, in collaboration with DocNet, hosted an event
on "Impact Research" with world-renowned expert Prof. Edward Freeman, facilitated by Prof.
Julian Kölbel.

During the discussion, Ed reflected on his own journey in impact research, sharing insights
into the key factors that shaped his pathway. He provided valuable advice on navigating the
challenges of academic publishing and building a meaningful academic career. Among his key
recommendations, he emphasized the importance of pursuing research that genuinely interests us
and keeping our own unique voice in academic writing.

A special thank you to Prof. Julian Kölbel for his excellent facilitation and to all
participants for their enthusiastic engagement in making this session both inspiring and
insightful!

### Public lecture by guest professor Penny Dick: The (in)visibility of inequality: Rethinking equality and diversity in organizations

3 April 2025, 12:00–14:00 · SQUARE Arena

In this talk, guest professor Penny Dick unpacks why the visibility of work is key to
understanding issues around equality and diversity in organizations. In her presentation, she
sheds light on valuation practices and explores how the invisibility of some work and the high
visibility of other work is created. Questioning why only specific contributions are perceived
as valuable and significant, her analysis paves the way for a more inclusive understanding of
diversity that recognizes and honours all forms of contribution, both visible and invisible,
thereby fostering an environment where true equality can flourish.

Penny Dick, PhD, is a professor at
[Sheffield University](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/management/people/academic-staff/penny-dick)
in the UK. This spring term, she is collaborating with our university as a guest professor at
the School of Humanities and Social Sciences. Penny Dick is a critical management scholar
interested in inequalities in employment and careers. She has published widely and serves as
Co-editor-in-chief of Human Relations. Her presentation is grounded in her most recent book on
"[Rethinking Gender Inequality in Organizations](https://www.elgaronline.com/monobook-oa/book/9781802207385/9781802207385.xml)".

### Workshop: Publishing in Academic Journals with Prof. Penny Dick

3 April 2025, 14:15–16:45 · A 01-206

In this publication workshop, we will make use of Prof Emma Bell's practical approach to
academic writing which she refers to as "concept notes". This is a structured method for
helping you identify and articulate issues such as developing a cogent research question;
thinking through your theoretical framework/approach; building the case for your study; and
being clear about the contribution you are making. In addition to discussing how to use
concept notes, we will discuss targeting appropriate journals and I will give a brief
presentation of what the journal Human Relations expects in its submitted papers.

**How to register?** Participants are encouraged to prepare for the workshop by completing a
concept note assignment beforehand. You will receive the assignment upon registering. Please
register for the workshop by emailing Julia Nentwich.

### Sprache wirkt – Inklusiv und wertschätzend kommunizieren

22 April 2025 and 25 September 2025, 09:30–12:00 · SQUARE

Unsere Sprache formt unsere Haltung, unser Miteinander und unsere Kultur. Mit achtsamer
Sprache schaffen wir ein respektvolles Umfeld, in dem sich alle Menschen gleichermassen
angesprochen und wertgeschätzt fühlen.

In diesem Seminar erfährst du, wie Sprache Vielfalt sichtbar macht, Missverständnisse
vermeidet und eine inklusive Kommunikation stärkt. Du lernst praxiserprobte Strategien, um
geschlechtergerechte und diskriminierungsfreie Sprache verständlich, flüssig und natürlich in
deinen Alltag zu integrieren.

Deine Lernziele:

- ✅ Bewusstsein für die Wirkung und Verantwortung von Sprache
- ✅ Grundlagen und Prinzipien inklusiver und geschlechtergerechter Kommunikation
- ✅ Praktische Umsetzungsmöglichkeiten für eine respektvolle, klare und zugängliche Sprache

Nutze die Kraft der Sprache – für ein wertschätzendes und faires Miteinander!

Kursleitung:
[Dr. Anna-Kathrin Heydenreich](https://www.unisg.ch/de/universitaet/ueber-uns/organisation/detail/person-id/883569d7-63d5-4730-9f03-f32417b5ca7a/)

### Online event: Design Practice in a Time Between Worlds

9 April 2025, 19:00–21:00 · online

Join this online free event on Rethinking design:

We live in an era of hyper-specialization. Professions are so narrowly defined that most
people no longer feel responsible for reshaping the rules of the game. Governance, economy,
and social structures are treated as someone else's job. But the systemic challenges we
face—climate adaptation, social equity, bioregional transition—cannot be solved within
isolated silos.

We need a postdisciplinary approach, where design is not just about products, spaces, and
services, but about reconfiguring the systems that shape our world.
