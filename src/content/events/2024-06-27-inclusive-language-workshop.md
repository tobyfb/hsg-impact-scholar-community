A workshop on how to use inclusive language in academic work: in papers, presentations,
lectures and daily life. In a safe space, participants reflected on the importance of our
use of language to mitigate the reproduction of bias. The workshop was delivered by the HSG
Diversity and Inclusion office with community member Dr. Anna-Katrin Heydenreich.
