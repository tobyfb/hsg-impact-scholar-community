A discussion of the challenges associated with AI in society, and of approaches that digital
ethics and human rights offer to understand and address them. Team members of the Institute
for Business Ethics (IWE-HSG) gave short inputs as a basis for discussions in a world café
format. Participants brought their own research, interests and perspectives.

The inputs:

- Florian Krause on the ethical challenges of metaphors in the context of AI
- Ingrid Becker on opportunity and trust issues related to AI and blockchain
- Kebene Wodajo on structural injustice in the context of AI
- Mariam Shakil on technological solutionism
- Thorsten Busch on human perception and the hype around AI

## Background

AI is said to have the potential to contribute to solving some of the world's grand
challenges. Yet concerns about the conditions and actual adverse impacts of AI on society
and the planet are emerging. They include data bias and gender data gaps, structural changes
in the job market, privacy, fair, transparent and trustworthy AI techniques, accountability,
and the potential for misuse, for example for gender-based violence or surveillance, and the
deepening of existing inequalities.
