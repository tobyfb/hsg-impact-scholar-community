To kick off the community semester: a workshop with community member Prof. Sebastian
Kernbach, founder of the Life Design Lab. Building on life design and design thinking, the
Sustainable Development Goals and the Inner Development Goals, he facilitated an afternoon
on "how to drive what matters to you, create impact and bring more of yourself alive".

The apéro afterwards was an opportunity to catch up and get to know new people. The workshop
was first announced for 10 May 2023 and then moved to the start of the autumn semester.
