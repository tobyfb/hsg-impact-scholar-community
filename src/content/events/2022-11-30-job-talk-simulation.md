## Title

Temporal dynamics in climate change disclosure: the interplay of consistency and
accountability in greenhouse gas target setting

## Abstract

Sustainability disclosure is a temporal process that builds on past commitments toward the
future. However, stakeholders discuss its quality solely based on a current snapshot. To
understand how sustainability disclosure changes with time, we tracked Greenhouse (GHG)
emissions reduction targets of 105 S&P companies across six sectors. Our findings reveal a
set of intra-firm temporal comparability issues. Across all industries, more than 50% of the
targets were either set in hindsight, shifted, or disbanded. We use these findings to develop
a typology of temporal comparability comprised of consistency and accountability, thereby
adding a temporal dimension to the greenwashing and impression management literature. We
provide recommendations for practitioners, policymakers, ESG agencies, and the public on
holding companies accountable for their past commitments.
