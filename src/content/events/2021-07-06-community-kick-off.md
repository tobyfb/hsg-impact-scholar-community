Two months after the [initial invitation](/about/#how-it-started), the community met for the
first time. In a World Café, participants worked on three questions: what impact is, what
they expect from the community and can contribute to it, and how the community should
organise itself.

## Looking back

These are the outputs of the three World Café tables.

### 1. Impact

- What is impact?
  - Have a positive contribution to society, nature and the entire ecosystem
  - Reach society with academic insights (science communication), and vice versa: include
    practical insights into academia; have impact with those insights in the world and in
    practice
  - Real-world problem-driven research
  - Co-create knowledge and impact with practice (see also action research)
  - Impact = ideas + execution (or observe > create > execute)
  - Dimensions of impact: through research, through teaching, on cause or activism, social
    media
  - Consider all effects and all parties involved (human and non-human)
- Why is it important to me?
  - Use my time, education and privilege wisely
  - Enhance well-being
  - Give me a purpose and self-efficacy (do something!)
  - Initiate others to start re-thinking; start a movement
- How can I create it?
  - Change culture; provide an entertaining platform
  - Involvement; non-academic formats to reach the public
  - Real-world problem-driven research
  - Work interdisciplinary
  - Changing incentive structures and systems
  - Embed human and emotional aspects (speak limbic)

### 2. Expectations and contributions

- What are my needs and desires to be addressed by the Impact Scholar Community?
  - Exchange forum; building bridges across institutions; overview of activities at HSG
  - Meet people and exchange
  - Support group, writing group, inspiration
  - Systems thinking and system dynamics
  - Co-authors; sparring partners
- Why am I here today?
  - Curiosity
  - Leveraging positive impact
  - Inspiration
  - See different perspectives
  - Exchange
  - Kick-start post-doc life
  - Find common interests and potential collaboration partners
- What can I contribute to the Impact Scholar Community?
  - Links to scholars and ideas
  - Ideas that might, or will definitely, work
  - Practical lens
  - Contacts to corporates
  - (Research) experience: social movements, practice research, environmental data science,
    sustainability consulting, multi-stakeholder open innovation processes and more
  - Being a conversation and collaboration partner

### 3. Organisation and agenda

- How do we want to organise as a community?
  - Let's build a community!
  - Get inspired through mutual exchange!
  - Find sparring partners
  - Create positive impact and translate research insights outside of academia
  - Have a virtual space for exchange (documents and communication)
  - Many different communication channels, services and tools were discussed
  - Meet in person regularly, once a month
  - Bigger and smaller meetings
  - Develop a backlog of topics and a voting system to select topics
  - Share participant lists and results of meetings
- What formats would be cool?
  - Have a good mix of events and formats
  - Guest speakers
  - (Paper) presentations and discussions
  - Workshops, exchanges, community meetings
  - Bounce research ideas
  - Newsletter
  - Science slams
  - Brownbag lunches
  - "Light style", e.g. my topic in 180 seconds or on one page
  - Paper collaborations
  - Art and exhibition projects
  - For research: find co-authors and get new insights faster, share data sets, get and give
    feedback from an outside perspective, leverage each other's networks
- What topics do we want to discuss?
  - We did not really talk a lot about topics yet.
