The community celebrates its fifth anniversary with a Methods Jam: a chance to explore fresh
ways to do sustainability and impact-oriented research, with insights into diverse research
methods and opportunities to discuss them. The event was co-organised with Prof. Judith
Ströhle and is followed by an apéro.

The inputs:

- Prof. Judith Ströhle: engaged scholarship
- Prof. Charlotta Sirén: geospatial data
- Dr. Tobias Fehr-Bosshard and Clémentine Robert: action research
- Jacob Eichberger: eye-tracking

## Looking back

We celebrated our fifth anniversary and got great feedback on the Methods Jam. A shorter
edition follows towards the end of the year: the
[Mini Methods Jam](/events/2026-11-30-mini-methods-jam/) on 30 November 2026.
