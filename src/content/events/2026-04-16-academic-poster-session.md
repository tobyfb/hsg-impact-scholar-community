The fifth edition of the Academic Poster Session at SQUARE, organised together with the
Sustainability Services team. The session is a platform to present current research projects
on topics around ethics, responsibility, sustainability and impact scholarship in general.
PhD students, postdocs and faculty members are encouraged to present a poster about a recent
publication or a work in progress, even if they cannot attend the session in person.

New this year is a second track for posters on innovative teaching formats, to create more
visibility and awareness for that impact pathway.

The costs of printing the posters are covered, and drinks and snacks are provided. After the
session, the posters are displayed in the main building for two weeks. The exhibition is open
to all students and employees of the University of St.Gallen as well as to the interested
public.
