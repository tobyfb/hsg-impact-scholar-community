The second workshop of the Impact Research Exchange Series. The team of Prof. Jamie Gloor and
other HSG researchers pitched their projects on social sustainability, followed by a world
café on questions related to impact research: how to create impact and how to move a
research project forward.

## Looking back

Prof. Jamie Gloor and her team hosted the Impact Scholar Community for a research exchange.
We listened to seven inspiring research pitches and had fruitful discussions. It was a
valuable, enriching opportunity to connect across centers, institutes and faculties on
social sustainability. Thanks again to all participants and presenters, and a special thanks
to Jamie and Eugenia for co-organizing the event.
