We are facing multiple ecological and social crises. Business schools influence our thinking
and how our socio-economic systems function. They can play an important role in tackling
grand challenges through research, teaching and collaboration. This workshop asked what HSG
specifically and concretely could contribute in this "Decade of Action".

Different stakeholders in the HSG ecosystem gave short vision statements of a more impactful
future. Prof. Maja Göpel, transformation researcher and sustainability expert, who was at HSG
as Personality in Residence at SQUARE, added an outside-in perspective. Inspired by these
outlooks, a world café served to co-create and discuss actionable ideas for developing and
scaling HSG's positive impact. Students, faculty, administration and everyone interested
were invited.
