The first workshop on quantitative sustainability research at HSG, organised by Prof. Judith
Stroehle and Prof. Florian Eugster. It brought together PhD students, postdocs and faculty
members at the University of St.Gallen who are involved in quantitative research on
sustainability topics. The workshop was meant to raise interdisciplinary awareness of
similar research and to create a network of researchers with shared interests. ACA-HSG and
the Impact Scholar Community co-sponsored coffee and the apéro.

The papers discussed:

- Stefan Bühler: Product Design and Consumer Participation in Circular Business Models
- Stefano Ramelli: Inflation, the Corporate Greed Narrative, and the Value of CSR
- Jamie Gloor: From Egocentric to Ecocentric (and vice versa)?
- Dennis Fehrenbacher: Effect of CSR Information Presentation Order on Decision-Making
- Miron Avidan: (In)-consistencies in Corporate GHG Emissions Reduction Targets
- Kerrigan Unter: Adaptation strategies to the external biophysical environment as a source
  of competitive advantage: The California wine industry
