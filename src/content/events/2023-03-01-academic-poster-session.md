As part of the oikos Sustainability Week 2023, the Delegate's team for Responsibility and
Sustainability organised an academic poster session to present current research projects in
the field of sustainability. PhD students, postdocs and faculty members were invited to
present a poster about their research project. The Delegate's team sponsored the printing of
the posters as well as drinks and snacks.

## Looking back

37 posters on current sustainability-related research were presented. It was fantastic to
connect with so many of you and to discuss your research projects.
