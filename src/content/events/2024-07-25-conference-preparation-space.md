Quite a few community members were accepted for presentations at AOM 2024 in Chicago in
August, some of them attending and presenting at AOM or another academic conference for the
first time. This session is a space to prepare for and exchange about AOM and academic
conferences more broadly. In the "Mock AOM" session, attendees test their presentations and
get feedback from peers and faculty. The idea is that this is not only a way to prepare
conference contributions individually but also an opportunity to get to know other HSG
people and their research.

The session is for conference presenters, first-time conference attendees, experienced
presenters and everything in between.

It was first announced as "Mock AOM 2024 session" for 13 June 2024, with an additional part
planned under the title "What I wish I had known during my first AOM", in which experienced
members share key learnings and best practices for attending conferences in general and AOM
in particular.
