An introduction to the
[Ownership Project](https://www.hbs.edu/bigs/about/research/the-ownership-project) by Nien-hê
Hsieh, followed by a discussion with attendees. Nien-hê Hsieh is the Kim B. Clark Professor
of Business Administration in the General Management Unit at Harvard Business School. His
research and teaching aim at helping business leaders and organizations determine and deliver
on their responsibilities.

The Institute of Business Ethics (IWE-HSG) co-organised the event with the HSG Impact Scholar
Community on the occasion of his visit to St.Gallen.
