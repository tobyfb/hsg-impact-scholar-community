Authorship decisions, who is named and in which order, have major implications for academic
careers in every field, but they are often made in opaque and inconsistent ways. Power
dynamics, disciplinary norms and unclear expectations can lead to misunderstandings,
authorship conflict and even potential harm to early-career researchers.

This interactive workshop with Dr. Mary Hausfeld, held together with the Early Career
Program, explores the challenges and complexities of authorship and academic collaboration,
focusing on fairness, transparency and navigating complex power dynamics. It is followed by
an apéro and the [community semester kick-off](/events/2025-09-24-semester-kick-off/).
