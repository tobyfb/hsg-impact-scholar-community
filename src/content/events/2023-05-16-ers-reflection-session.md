Some community members took part in the PhD course "Ethics, Responsibility & Sustainability
(ERS) in Research and the PhD Journey" in the autumn semester 2022. One exercise they found
meaningful was an ERS reflection session: in small groups of three to five people, a short
but deep conversation of 10 to 15 minutes for each person. This prototype brings the practice
to the community, to find out whether there is interest in regular sessions once or twice a
semester.

## The questions

1. Where do I come from? How did I end up here? What drives me?
2. Why did I pick my PhD topic?
3. Which ERS topics are relevant for my research?
4. How can I make an impact in practice?
5. What do I need from my peers?
6. How do I position myself within my field of research?
