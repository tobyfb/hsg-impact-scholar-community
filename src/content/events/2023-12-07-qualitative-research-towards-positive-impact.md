A session on the process of conducting qualitative empirical research on sustainability
topics, with perspectives on ethnography, interviews and case studies, followed by a
discussion and Q&A. The event was co-organised with community member Prof. Paula Bialski,
who presented her ethnographic research "Hacking the Climate: researching climate activism
online and off".
