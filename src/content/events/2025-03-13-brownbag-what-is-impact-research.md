A discussion on impact research with Prof. Edward Freeman, a pioneer in stakeholder theory,
led by Prof. Julian Kölbel. The format is a brownbag lunch combined with a fireside chat,
followed by a discussion in groups on what impact research means, why impact is needed, and
how young scholars can have a positive impact with their research insights. The event is open
to students, faculty, PhDs and anyone interested in impact research. Lunch is provided.

## Looking back

On 13 March, the HSG Impact Scholar Community, in collaboration with DocNet, hosted an event
on "Impact Research" with Prof. Edward Freeman, facilitated by Prof. Julian Kölbel.

During the discussion, Ed reflected on his own journey in impact research, sharing insights
into the key factors that shaped his pathway. He provided valuable advice on navigating the
challenges of academic publishing and building a meaningful academic career. Among his key
recommendations, he emphasized the importance of pursuing research that genuinely interests
us and keeping our own unique voice in academic writing.

A special thank you to Prof. Julian Kölbel for his excellent facilitation and to all
participants for their enthusiastic engagement in making this session both inspiring and
insightful!
