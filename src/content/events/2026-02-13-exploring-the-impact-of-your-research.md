A virtual event series and reflective space for HSG impact scholars. In spring 2026, the HSG
Impact Scholar Community connects with the global
[AOM Impact Scholar Community](https://www.impactscholarcommunity.com/) for virtual sessions
on different aspects of research impact.

Each session features a live panel with international researchers, followed by a short
interactive Q&A. Afterwards, HSG participants join a dedicated online space to go deeper into
the topic, exchange experiences, reflect on challenges and support one another in exploring
how to address the topic in their own work. The series is for scholars who are just starting
out as well as for those who are already engaged scholars.

## Dates and topics

- 13 February 2026, 16:00–17:30: "Engage with the Public: Connect with Communities that
  benefit from your research", for researchers interested in engaging with the public through
  public communications. Guests: Andrew Crane (University of Bath), Carmen Quinonez
  (University of Michigan) and HSG Impact Scholar Community member Jamie Gloor (University of
  St.Gallen).
- 17 April 2026, 17:30–19:00: "Teach for Impact: Leverage pedagogy and mentorship to drive
  local change", for researchers interested in impactful teaching and mentorship. Guests: Kam
  Phung (Simon Fraser University), Kate Odziemkowska (University of Toronto) and Steve
  Kennedy (Erasmus University).
- 11 June 2026, 16:00–17:30: "Shape the Future: Identify emerging challenges and address them
  through responsive research", for researchers interested in addressing emerging and
  evolving societal issues. Guests: Ali Aslan Gümüsay (University of Munich), Grace Augustine
  (University of Bath) and Oana Branzei (Western University).
