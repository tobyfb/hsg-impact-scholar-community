As part of the oikos Sustainability Week, HSG and OST co-organised an Academic Poster
Session. It is a place to present research on the SDGs or a related topic, to exchange ideas
with colleagues and to find opportunities for future research collaborations.

Posters can cover a recent publication or work in progress, and can be contributed even by
those who cannot join the session in person. The format is A0 portrait, with the research
title, background, methods, results, and the contribution to theory and practice of the
project.
