The kick-off follows the workshop
["Who gets credit?"](/events/2025-09-24-who-gets-credit-authorship-workshop/) on authorship.
Besides the apéro, it is a moment to (re-)connect by sharing a bit about personal
developments and achievements of the previous academic year and by setting intentions for
what is to come, in an optional and joyful way. Participants are invited to bring an artifact
to get conversations started, for example a printed dissertation or article, or a picture
from a workshop or presentation at AOM or EGOS.

There is also an opportunity to help co-create the future of the Impact Scholar Community.
