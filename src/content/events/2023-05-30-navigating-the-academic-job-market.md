An event to support early-career academics in finding a job, co-organised with community
member Dr. Kerrigan Unter. The session covered challenges, pitfalls, tips and resources that
are needed to successfully start an academic career in the field of sustainability.
Kerrigan Unter gave an input, followed by a panel discussion in which researchers shared
their experiences and answered questions from the audience.
