Another opportunity to meet and exchange within the community. At the Holiday Event,
community members share gifts in the form of tools, methods or general tips for impact
research (and life): an inspiring paper, an interesting method or a tool such as Research
Rabbit or Obsidian. The format: very short pitches of two to four minutes, then World
Café-style discussions. More material gifts, such as cookies, are welcome too.

Afterwards there is mulled wine ("Glühwein") on the terrace. The same evening, from 18:00,
Prof. Alexander Braun gives his inaugural lecture on "Mitigating Climate Change and its
Impact: The Responsibility and Role of the Insurance Industry", which those who want can
join.
