An in-person get-together of the Impact Scholar Community. Participants illustrated their
research and discussed their work with each other.

## Looking back

During our get-together on 8 March, we illustrated our research and discussed our work. It
was a great way to get the conversation started and see links between each other's work in a
very short amount of time. We appreciated the in-person get-together and discussions after a
rather long time of just seeing people in Zoom windows.
