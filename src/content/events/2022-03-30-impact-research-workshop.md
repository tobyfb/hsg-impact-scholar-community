The first community workshop, and the start of a series in which a chair or institute hosts
the community. The research team around Prof. Judith Walls pitched their research projects,
and participants discussed questions related to impact research in a world café. The aim: to
connect with sustainability scholars, learn about their research and get inspiration for
your own work.

## Looking back

The team of Prof. Judith Walls presented their research projects, including sustainability
branding, sustainability strategies, circular economy, sustainable leadership,
sustainability and institutional complexity, business and biodiversity, employees' green
creativity, and trophy hunting. After the research pitches, we had a deep dive into specific
challenges and questions that each researcher is facing. It was a lively and fruitful
exchange.
