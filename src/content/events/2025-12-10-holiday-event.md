The traditional Holiday Event is a last opportunity to connect in the old year. As every
year, it is a space to share gratitude and the things people are currently excited by, in
work, research and life.

The event is also an experiment in collaboratively planning the year 2026 for the HSG Impact
Scholar Community. By now there are a few established formats (semester openings, the
Academic Poster Session, the Conference Practice Space and Hosted-by events), and always the
hope to co-create new ones. The questions: what would you be particularly interested in, and
is there something you might commit to co-organising, contributing to or shaping in 2026?

Finally, the community says goodbye to Eugenia Bajet Mestre, who has been a key collaborator
in developing the community over the last few years. There is Glühwein, non-alcoholic punch
and snacks. The event is also meant as an opportunity to get a sense of the community for
anyone who is interested in impact scholarship but has not taken part before.
