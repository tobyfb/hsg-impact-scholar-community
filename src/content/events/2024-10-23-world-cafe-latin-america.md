As part of the Latin American Week, the HSG Impact Scholar Community organised a World Café.
HSG researchers studying topics in the context of Latin America present their research in
short pitches of five to ten minutes and then engage in group discussion.

The programme:

- Colombian coffee degustation
- Discussion with social and environmental leaders of the Colombian Inga indigenous people
- World Café: short research presentations and dialogue on doing sustainability research on
  Latin America with Prof. Dr. Thomas Grisaffi
