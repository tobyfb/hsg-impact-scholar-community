An event organised by the HSG Impact Scholar Community and ECOnnect to connect students and
scholars on impact research. Scholars can find support for research projects, while students
can get guidance for writing impactful theses. Scholars from different institutes and centers
showcase their research topics and the opportunities to write a thesis with them, in a
five-minute pitch with one slide per researcher. The objective is to bring together students
and scholars at HSG, united by a shared interest in creating positive societal and
environmental impact through research.

The event was envisioned as a series in two parts. Part 1, on 3 March 2025, aims to inspire
students and give them an overview of potential topics and supervisors for Bachelor's,
Master's or doctoral theses in the impact field at HSG. Part 2 was planned for fall 2025: an
event at which students who have finished or are finishing their thesis present their key
findings to the community.
