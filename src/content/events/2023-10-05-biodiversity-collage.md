In a Biodiversity Collage, participants playfully learn about the role of biodiversity in
maintaining the balance of ecosystems and about ways to take action, while they connect with
like-minded people from HSG and Switzerland. Participation fees were covered by ECOnnect.

- Workshop, 5 October 2023, 17:00–20:00, SQUARE: the systemic aspects of biodiversity in a
  collaborative workshop. What is biodiversity? What enables and what degrades it?
- Facilitator training, 23 November 2023, 17:00–20:00, SQUARE Wintergarten: the skills and
  know-how to facilitate Biodiversity Collages. Trained facilitators can run Biodiversity
  Collage workshops for friends, colleagues and family by themselves.
